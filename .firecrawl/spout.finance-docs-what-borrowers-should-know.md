# What Borrowers Should Know

You are exposed to two things while borrowing: the price of your underlying collateral (just as you would be if you simply held it), and the chance of assignment in any given cycle. Assignment is bounded; the most assignment can do is cap your upside at the strike for that one cycle. The protocol does not introduce any new failure mode that does not already exist for someone who simply holds the underlying share.
